## NSF OISE Award Browser

This R Shiny app makes NSF Office of International Science and Engineering award data interactively explorable across four core programs -- PIRE, IRES, AccelNet, and Global Centers. Users can filter by year, program, and EPSCoR status to examine geographic distribution, funding amounts, and temporal trends in OISE investments. The app surfaces structural patterns in federal international science funding, including program-level disparities in award size and frequency and the underrepresentation of EPSCoR jurisdictions in high-value awards.
